# Library surface

Mode: Operate (finding lessons), Read (lesson descriptions). User delegated minor implementation choices and explicitly requested sharing the direction then continuing without approval. Additional skill interviews, random styling choices and a blocking decision board are therefore omitted in favor of that current request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A two-part teaching collection with the shortest visible path from a lesson to its resources. No promotional hero or invented results.

OWN-WORLD: Bright near-white, dark green-black text, forest green selection, thin neutral rules. Korean sans, restrained rectangular lesson cards, text-led navigation. No decorative images or motion.

STORY: See the two parts, narrow the lesson list, open a real lesson, understand the availability immediately, then read the teaching context.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact wordmark and navigation, a short collection introduction beside two part jump links, preparation notice, labeled search and native selects, then the first part heading and lesson cards. No graphic pushes the lesson list down.

FORM: Educational archive catalog, from the reference principles of arte Library, MoMA's terms index and It's Nice That. Seven considered systems: public library, curriculum guide, exhibition index, research journal, educational archive, museum guide, lesson-study handout. Candidate 5; seed:e3bde014df5392fb. User task requirements govern composition and styling.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
